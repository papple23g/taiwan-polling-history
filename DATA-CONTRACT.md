# Public data contract

Only public and explicitly provenance-labeled evidence belongs in `public/data/verified.json`. The released dataset contains verified primary records AND clearly flagged pending records. Pending records may show provisional arithmetic, but never count as verified analysis. Each record retains public source evidence and the exact verification tier. Test fixtures live under `tests/` and must never be copied to the public bundle.

Additional public collections: `historicalContexts` and `heldRecords` retain original candidate scenarios and source conflicts, each with public `sources`, original support and date observations, reason, pending status and `officialScoreEligible: false`. They never enter `polls` or scoring.

Top-level fields: `version` (string), `updatedAt` (ISO date), `elections` (array), `institutions` (array), `polls` (array), `corrections` (array).

Election: `id`, `label`, `year`, `date` (YYYY-MM-DD), `region`, `office`, `status` (`verified` or `pending`), `candidates` (`id`, `name`, optional `party`, `color`), optional `comparisonCandidateIds` (explicit matched subset, otherwise all candidates), and `result` with `status`, `votes` (candidate-id map of nonnegative numbers or null), `validVotes`, and `source`.

Institution: `id`, original `brand`, optional `executor` and `teamNote`. Each poll separately records `commissioner`, `executor`, `funder`, `designAndAnalysis`, `teamId` and `teamNote`. The original brand may describe a publisher or another recorded role; it is not used to infer a missing executor or a permanent team.

The comparison UI groups poll brands through src/publisher-brand.mjs using original recorded brands, sourced name aliases and per-poll attribution rules without rewriting the dataset. A brand may name a publisher, commissioner, party, media outlet or survey organization; it is not universally treated as a verified publisher or executor. Shared executors do not merge brands, and missing executors do not exclude otherwise calculable records. Each brand/election selects at most one recorded calculable poll with a public source and verified official result, including pending records. Verification tier, sample and method completeness do not gate inclusion. Original poll and institution IDs and verification fields remain intact. Each row retains its included per-election scores in `availablePerElection`, without requiring a universal common set. Each brand average is the equal-weight mean of its own included elections, calculable with one or more elections. `averageCount` and `averageElectionIds` identify that row's included set. `median` is the middle per-election MAE, or the mean of the middle pair for an even count; `maximum` is the largest included per-election MAE. All three statistics use unrounded scores in percentage points, preserve zero, and are null for no included elections. The view defaults to requiring at least five included elections under the current time filter. Disabling that condition puts all brands, including those with zero included elections, in the same table. Five is a display threshold, not a statistical reliability guarantee. The unfiltered model retains all brands in `rows`, partitioned into `mainRows` and `limitedRows` using `mainTableMinimum`. `filterComparisonBrands` projects the visible brand rows without changing any statistics. Rows are sorted by unrounded average ascending, with missing averages last and stable order for ties. The comparison content contains the two existing filters and a table with six columns in this order: 機構, 平均誤差(MAE), 採計場次, 中位數, 誤差分佈 and 詳情. Median is shown in the main table; median and maximum values also remain in the brand drawer. Explanatory definitions remain in the methodology dialog. Numeric headers, units, scores and score buttons align right; institution and text labels align left. Single-election labels use 誤差(MAE), while methodology retains the full MAE definition. The single-election date heading, compact label and sort option use 調查日期, displaying and sorting the survey-start date in `fieldworkStart`; the `date` sort key remains unchanged. Missing or invalid start dates remain unknown. Sets may differ across brands, so ordering does not establish an equal-coverage ranking or institutional credibility. These statistics describe the historical errors of published results involving each brand; they do not isolate the effects of questionnaire design, weighting, commissioning or publication decisions.

The distribution is derived from the unrounded MAEs in that row's `availablePerElection`, using 2-percentage-point bins from zero. Bins include their lower bound and exclude their upper bound, except that the last bin includes the shared upper bound. A true zero belongs in the first bin; a row with zero included elections has no distribution. Each bar is the bin's election count divided by the brand's included election count, with a fixed 0–100% Y axis. A vertical dashed line marks the unrounded median. Each bin tooltip lists the full election names and individual MAEs for that bin as text, without links or actions. All brands share an X-axis upper bound: the largest included per-election MAE across every brand under the current date condition, before applying the minimum-five-election display filter, rounded up to a multiple of ten and at least 30 percentage points. Toggling the five-election threshold does not change the scale. The histogram describes recorded coverage; neither its shape nor its election count establishes statistical reliability or equal-coverage comparison.

Clicking a distribution chart or 詳情 opens a right-side modal brand drawer, 600px wide when space permits. It contains median and maximum values, a larger histogram, expandable bin values, per-election details (election, survey-end date, MAE, verification status and source), and expandable exclusions. Unknown survey-end dates remain unknown. Per-election details follow the shared year/region order; clicking a per-election MAE enters that election's history and selects the exact poll ID. Drawer and distribution-tooltip state is transient, excluded from URLs and history snapshots, and closes on refresh, popstate or leaving comparison. Below 940px of content width, brands use multiline rows; at 375px the comparison does not require horizontal scrolling. The drawer uses the same included scores and original verification fields as the summary row.

Poll: `id`, `electionId`, `institutionId`, `fieldworkStart`, `fieldworkEnd`, `publishedAt`, `status` (`verified` or `pending`), `source`, `shares` (candidate-id map, percentage points 0–100 or null), `undecided` (number or null), `other` (number or null), `isExplicitFinal` (boolean), optional `closingEvidence` (public source proving final-poll wording), optional `sampleSize`, `method`, `notes`, `commissioner`, `executor`, `teamId`, `teamNote`.

Source tier: raw source records keep `verification_status='verified_primary'` or `verification_status='indexed_secondary'`, mapped to public `poll.status='verified'` or `poll.status='pending'`. The `provenance` object preserves the original source tier, raw table reference, and audit notes. Indexed rows may have `publishedAt: null` if publication date is not independently verified. Pending may have original full text, news, secondary-table or search-excerpt evidence. The legacy enum `indexed_secondary` means pending verification; it does not assert that every source is secondary. `historical_import.evidence_tier` and each source access method describe the actual evidence. The existing verified-score contract requires primary evidence for six core fields (support, fieldwork end, pre-election publication, executor, sample size and method) and consistency checks; pending `evaluation.eligible` stays false and `evaluation.mae` stays null. This contract is independent of the brand comparison, which uses `evaluation.calculation.mae` and includes calculable pending records. `sourceCalculation` is retained for provenance; arithmetic is recomputed from recorded inputs through the shared function. Inclusion never promotes a record's verification status. Pending records remain visible in the single-election closing/history views and exports.

Source: `title`, `url` (public https/http URL), optional `publishedAt`, `accessedAt`.

`verificationReview` is independent of `poll.status` and arithmetic: `auditId`, `status` (`primary_verified`, `secondary_cross_checked`, `partial`, `blocked`, `conflict`), `checkedAt`, `reason`, `verifiedFields`, `missingFields`, and `fieldEvidence` keyed by input field. Each field locator has a public `url`, `locator`, `checkedAt`, and optional `note`. It also preserves `originalPublicRecordId`, original observations, per-field corrections, conflicts, recovery attempts, and related record IDs. Primary promotions and conflict adjudications require an approved independent content reread. Two independent complete secondary sources may establish `secondary_cross_checked`; copied wire/press-release text is one source family and does not qualify. This secondary status does not grant an official score.

`calculatePollError(poll, election)` is the shared pure arithmetic function. It normalizes recorded support and official integer-vote distributions separately over the declared comparison set. It returns `calculable`, `mae`, both normalized distributions, `reason`, and `warnings`. It does not require known dates, samples, method, executor, or verification. Incompatible candidate scenarios, missing support, invalid votes, non-voting-intention products, and explicit unresolved support-input conflicts are not calculable. `evaluatePoll` preserves official `eligible`/`mae` semantics and adds `calculation`, `provisionalMae`, `calculationReason`, and `calculationWarnings`. Pending official `mae` remains null. A reported candidate/undecided/other total over 100.5% may retain a provisional MAE with a warning; the official consistency gate remains strict. The original-percentage display never changes either normalized score. The main table defaults to numeric MAE ascending order; unavailable values remain last. Verification details remain available in the selected record's notes and sources.

Historical candidate contexts are never scored, even after primary source verification. Unknown fieldwork dates display as unknown and are not assigned to the optional 30-day window.

Correction: `date`, `title`, `description`, optional `source`. Do not include private conversation or account information.

MAE is mean absolute difference in percentage points after both distributions are normalized over the exact same declared candidate IDs. Missing candidate support values are not calculable and are never treated as zero. Raw percentages and excluded candidates remain visible. Brand comparison excludes polls with a known fieldwork end or publication date on or after election day. The comparison date window is enabled by default; fieldwork must end 1–30 Taiwan calendar days before election day, before choosing the final/latest record. The single-election default remains disabled. Without the window, unknown dates may contribute without being filled from index dates. Explicit final status is accepted only with `closingEvidence`; otherwise choose the newest fieldwork end, then publication date, with unknown dates last. At the same priority, differing comparison-candidate support excludes the cell; matching support may use a stable representative despite metadata differences. A final poll need not be within seven days. No brand-specific outlier exclusions are applied. The independent verified-score contract retains its stricter date, consistency and exact-reprint checks.

Cross-election comparison takes one final/latest calculable record per brand/election, not all waves. The brand view uses each brand's own set with a calculation minimum of one election and a separate five-election main-table threshold. The lower-level `commonElectionComparison` function defaults to its verified shared-intersection contract with a minimum of three for reproducible audits. The brand view opts into `includeRecorded: true`, then computes its own-set statistics; it does not use the shared-intersection average. Per-election values, total coverage and excluded/missing elections are retained. `methodology.minimum_common_contests` describes the default audit API; `minimum_comparison_contests` describes the brand calculation minimum, not the main-table display threshold. Historical `common_contest_comparisons` remain source audit evidence, not current UI averages.

Calendar interpretation uses Asia/Taipei for timestamps. Date-only values remain calendar dates. The verified-score consistency gate requires reported non-null candidate values, undecided and other to sum to no more than 100.5%; the 0.5 percentage-point tolerance allows published rounding. Calculable values above this threshold retain their warning and may contribute to recorded comparisons. Do not invent missing categories to complete 100%.

Historical provenance: `indexDate` and `sourceDateText` are index observations, never substitute fieldwork or publication dates. Unknown executor remains null. `sampleSize` is the per-survey/locality total; `sampleSizeSupportQuestion` is the support question subgroup; `surveyTotalSampleSize` is a separate whole-survey or multi-city total; its precise denominator remains in `supportDenominator` and the field evidence. `otherResponses` preserves named residual categories alongside numeric `other`. `questionCandidateSet` preserves the full question options without inventing zero support for unreported candidates. Candidate spelling aliases preserve the original observations. Catalog accounting uses `auditDisposition` to retain the original audit partition even after adjudication.
# 選前選舉資料

`election.phase` 為 `completed` 或 `pre_result`；舊資料省略時視為 `completed`。階段與來源核實分開，不依目前日期自動轉換。選前 `result.status=pending`，`validVotes` 與每位候選人的票數均為 `null`；候選名單是已收錄題目所出現的人名，不宣稱完整官方登記名冊。

`normalizePollSupport` 獨立換算已列明比較集合的民調支持度。選前 `calculatePollError`、`evaluatePoll` 一律不給正式或試算 MAE，即使誤填票數或來源已核實也不能啟用結果比較。跨場比較的選舉與品牌範圍只從已完成選舉建立。

已完成選舉的雙人圖為一維散點圖，每筆民調一點。X 軸是 `comparisonCandidateIds` 第一人的正規化支持度，第二人等於 100% 減去 X；所有民調與結果星號皆位於 Y=0，不抖動、不堆疊位移。官方整數票數先換算未四捨五入比例，再對齊相同候選集合；一顆紅色星號標示結果，點到星號的水平距離等於雙人 MAE。50% 為平手標記；左側為第二人、右側為第一人的勝選區，採當次官方推薦政黨色及 22% 不透明度，互動時為 32%，與三角圖共用。不改候選人分界；資料點與星號優先命中。重疊紀錄可由表格逐筆選取。

一維與三人圖的民調點為 18px，統一藍色（#3279cb）與 0.75px 白色細框；點內可顯示一至兩字的品牌縮寫，「顯示名稱」預設開啟。調查結束日與選舉日按台灣日曆日計算差距，不透明度分為選前 1–7 天 100%、8–14 天 80%、15–30 天 60%、超過 30 天 40%。歷史圖日期未知、無效、選舉當日或選後的紀錄可保留支持度，改畫固定大小藍色空心點及細白色外暈，提示明列日期狀態；不用發布日補算。選前三人圖維持日期完整才畫點。選取及 hover 加強外框或陰影，保留大小與日期不透明度；官方星號固定 17px、完全不透明。兩人及三角區域共用一般 22%、互動 32% 填色。

觸控以至少22px半徑判定鄰近點，直接命中優先，其餘選最近者；拖曳、雙指手勢及取消事件不當作點選。指標能力改變時保留同一圖表與視野。

歷史及選前三角圖的頭像與姓名可啟動候選人支持度教學：滑鼠移入／移開、鍵盤聚焦／失焦或 Escape，以及觸控點按切換。教學暫時隱藏領先區及原格線，以該候選人的當次官方分類色填滿三角形；色深為絕對正規化支持度乘以 32% 不透明度，0% 為白色，不依視野重新拉伸。相鄰兩邊標示 0–100%、每 20% 一格，共同頂點僅標一次，等值線連接同支持度位置。縮放後以 `100 − sum(viewport.min)` 為跨度，取不小於跨度五分之一的最小 1–2–5 級距，僅畫可見範圍內的真實刻度。教學不改選取、資料或網址；手機點民調或官方星號可保留教學與該點提示，再點同一頭像、空白、圖外，或拖曳、縮放、取消觸控、resize、資料／選舉／圖型／候選集合改變時清除。漸層及等值線位於資料點下方且不接收事件。

已完成及選前選舉的雙人圖與三角圖在 history 檢視預設顯示「歷程路線」。依既有民調品牌歸戶及台灣調查開始日，將目前可繪製的同品牌點由舊到新連接；同日各點分岔，同日內不連接，開始日未知或無效不連線，座標完全相同的兩點不畫零長度箭頭。雙人圖向右的箭頭上彎、向左的箭頭下彎；弧線高度僅供區分方向，不代表支持度或兩次調查間的變化，所有資料點仍位於 Y=0。篩選後以可見點重建路線，不補造中間波次。選取已連線的點時，以紫色和白色襯線強調該品牌路線，並淡化其他路線；同品牌其他可見點加上紫色外框，包含開始日未知而未連線的點。這些點置於其他品牌的點和官方星號之上，箭頭置於這些點之上，所選點仍在最上層；所有資料點的大小與日期深淺不變。未連線的選取及官方結果不強調路線。線段與箭頭不接收事件，並在三角圖候選人教學期間保留。路線不表示題目、方法、樣本相同或包含所有波次，也不提供預測。

單場雙人及三人圖的時間播放器切至 history，保留機構與 30 天篩選，以調查開始日及實際天數比例回放可繪紀錄；開始日未知及不可繪紀錄不排入，並分別顯示數量。各品牌顯示截至播放日最新可繪日期的全部紀錄，同日多筆保留。時間軸標記每筆可播放民調的日期分布，密集時彙整數量；1× 全程按天數比例約 7.5 秒，可調速、暫停、拖曳及依日期查看筆數。播放器啟用期間，不論播放或暫停均隱藏表格與詳情；篩選變更會暫停並重建時間軸。已完成選舉涵蓋投票日，投票日前隱藏官方結果與 MAE；選前資料播放至最後一筆可繪民調且沒有結果。圖型與候選集合按完整篩選範圍固定，播放器啟用期間隱藏歷程路線並保留視野。

播放日期旁以台灣日曆日顯示距投票日的倒數天數，投票日顯示 0 天，投票日後顯示已過天數。各日期筆數清單、播放說明及未排入數量放在圖表下方。

播放器在單一底圖上移動資料點；單筆波次在 1× 每段 0.2 秒，以慢、快、慢轉場，時長除以速度倍率。同日多點交叉淡入淡出，不任意配對；資料密集時先完成目前轉場，再接當時最新目標。點圖後日期暫停，尚未完成的點仍以目前速度移至該日期的最新目標，正常 1× 最多約 0.4 秒、0.1× 最多約 4 秒。暫停後可用 hover 查看提示，觸控以點按查看；轉場中的提示不顯示支持度，到位才顯示真實數值。拖曳進度至指定日期即時呈現真實快照。轉場不表示兩次調查之間有觀測值或預測。切換至背景自動暫停，回到前景仍保持暫停，未完成動畫在前景繼續走完。播放器內即使系統啟用減少動態效果仍保留移動，播放器以外沿用該偏好。播放狀態不寫入網址或 history.state，不改原始紀錄、來源、評分或正式資格。

一維圖初始範圍納入目前可繪製民調、有效官方結果及 50%，兩側各加 `max(3, (最大值 − 最小值) × 0.12)` 個百分點留白，再限制於 0–100%。沒有民調時仍依官方結果與 50% 決定範圍；兩者只剩 50% 時顯示 47–53%。雙擊還原此局部範圍。只有水平縮放平移，X 限制 0–100%、最多 100 倍，Y 不移動。資料更新與視野識別分開；同場、同圖型、同候選組合下，波次、機構及30天篩選均保留視野，包含無點狀態。選取、排序及版面變更亦不重設。切換選舉、圖型或候選組合才重設，不快取各圖型視野；雙擊依目前資料還原範圍。

選前雙人圖共用一維散點圖：X 為固定比較兩人第一人的正規化支持度，每筆一點、Y=0。兩人支持度完整但調查日期未知或無效時仍畫空心點，30 天篩選時排除。支持度缺漏、候選情境不符或衝突者只留表格；不用發布日代填。沒有官方星號、結果參考線、MAE 或勝率。

2026 選前圖以 `comparisonCandidateIds` 固定主要兩人；篩選後可見紀錄中，任一其他候選人的單一原始支持度達 5% 即改固定三人三角圖。第三人取最高原始支持度，同值依較新有效調查結束日、發布日、候選人 ID 排序。門檻先於日期與畫點完整性檢查；未知不當零、不合計其他人或未表態。三角圖只正規化固定三人，缺任一支持度就不畫該筆；沒有完整點時仍保留三人圖。這項自動門檻不改表格比較集合、評分或匯出。

歷史雙人圖缺少完整核實官方結果時，保留有效民調支持度，但不畫結果星號或顯示距離分數。選前圖不顯示官方結果、結果參考線或 MAE；選前三角圖另以「支持度領先區」呈現三人比例中何者最高，並共用歷史三角區域幾何、分類色及互動，不表示勝率。繪圖不依正式評分資格，但仍排除非投票意向、候選情境不符與未解支持度輸入衝突。

選前資料保留原始與可用的正規化支持度，官方結果及正式與試算 MAE 維持空值；`null` 不轉為零。

2026 來源：`2026-poll-index.json` 保存索引公開57波的逐題數值、方法及原始連結；`2026-zhubei-primary.json` 保存竹北美麗島同波調查的問卷、發布結果與逐題選項；`2026-primary-checks.json` 保存局部核對欄位、來源定位、存取限制與發布日期差異；`2026-poll-supplement.json` 保存獨立回源的選定題目、原始選項、分母、題目處置及核對限制。竹北問卷及結果頁、同波新聞轉載都不重複計筆；知名度及當選看好度不作投票意向。選區明列主要人選、黨籍及職務，竹北市長與新竹縣長分開。索引中的推論出資者不轉為已知出資者。未完成一手核心欄位核實的紀錄維持待核實，初選、不同候選情境、不同受訪者分母或非投票意向題進入來源目錄。這批資料並非完整2026民調普查。

重建使用下方完整的 `npm run data:rebuild` 流程。2026 匯入器僅管理來源分片20至22，更新總收錄範圍，保留歷史分片及既有逐筆紀錄。`npm run audit:2026` 從首頁發現全部縣市（包含零筆及隱藏卡片），保存頁面時間、雜湊與逐欄差異；不可讀頁面列未知，不當零筆或宣稱資料移除。公開題目需在主表或來源目錄恰好對應一筆且選項一致；只有份數的隱藏紀錄沒有波次身分，不匯入。獨立回源結果與未確認線索另存 `2026-poll-gap-research.json`。只有主動更新索引時才執行 `node scripts/collect-2026-index.mjs`；此蒐集器需 Python 3.12 與 Beautiful Soup，網站執行及固定快照重建不需要 Python。

## Historical local-election supplements

2022、2018、2014 各22縣市長均保留官方完整名單、整數票數、推薦政黨及各場實際投票日；2022嘉義市使用12月18日重行選舉。`local-history-official.json` 保存官方清單、取用網址與內容雜湊；三份 `local-history-<year>.json` 分別保存完整來源表格轉錄、原始欄位觀察、逐欄定位、各縣市搜尋盤點及可讀性限制。索引表頭明載的完成日期可記為索引所報調查結束日，但不構成一手核實；語意不明日期只存索引觀察。沒有查得可收錄民調不等於沒有進行民調。

`node scripts/import-local-election-history.mjs` 管理帶 `local-election-history` owner 的第23至26分片，依序為新增選舉、民調、候選情境及衝突；不覆寫其他匯入器分片。既有ID、支持度、比較集合及核實狀態不變；來源修正名稱時可明列原 `comparisonUnitId` 以維持原機構 ID；重複來源逐筆對帳。新增場次固定比較官方得票前二名，再加其他得票率達5%者，以整數票數判定並固定集合；既有場次不套新門檻。缺比較人選時不補零，原始全部選項保留；不同候選情境及非投票意向題分列目錄。

固定快照重建使用 `npm run data:rebuild`，依序執行歷史匯入器、2026匯入器、本地方選舉增補匯入器、公開缺漏增補匯入器，再轉換公開資料。年度輸入為固定來源，不在建置時連網重抓；主動更新官方快照才執行 `node scripts/collect-local-history-official.mjs`。2026匯入器統計會納入其所屬分片之外的全部資料。完整來源、公開資料與runtime分檔共用既有生成流程及revision檢查。`scope.local_history_collection` 保存66場盤點與逐筆處置數量，不宣稱完整歷史民調普查。

`source-data/poll-gap-supplement-20261027.json` 保存各年份公開缺漏增補、逐欄更正及既有支持度題的分類校正。`scripts/import-poll-gap-supplement.mjs` 讀取完整編號分片後套用，不改寫原分片。更正必須吻合原值並附公開網址與定位；分類校正保留既有ID、原分類及來源層級。同品牌、同場、相同候選支持度與可對齊日期的重複來源不新增題目；不同候選集合、條件題與子母體分開保存。回收問卷數不作有效樣本數，未知日期與缺值保持空值。選前新增場次維持官方結果與MAE為空值；補入資料不自動取得一手核實資格。

一般重建不可在公開缺漏增補階段之後再單獨執行分片組裝，否則會覆蓋增補結果。更新固定輸入後應完成整條 `data:rebuild`，並驗證主表、情境目錄、衝突目錄與來源輸入的逐筆對帳。公開來源包只保留資料與可回讀依據，本機研究快照及工作報告不進公開封裝。

所有地方選舉年度共用顯示排序：六都先列臺北、新北、桃園、臺中、臺南、高雄；其餘依基隆、新竹縣、竹北、新竹市、宜蘭、苗栗、彰化、南投、雲林、嘉義縣、嘉義市、屏東、花蓮、臺東、澎湖、金門、連江。臺／台僅供比對，未知地區穩定置後。此順序只用於導覽與跨場逐場明細，不改民調排序及統計契約。

## Runtime loading and share URLs

verified.json and verified-source.json remain complete downloads. scripts/build-runtime-data.mjs derives app-index.json and elections/<id>.json from the same approved dataset. Index poll records retain all flat operational fields plus semantic provenance (product_type, candidate_set, question_candidate_set) and review calculation blockers; source references retain title, URL and available publication/access dates. Full source evidence, provenance, sourceCalculation, verificationReview and source-catalogue records live in election files. catalogueCounts records historicalContexts and heldRecords counts per election.

A SHA-256 revision over the canonical full-data JSON plus newline accompanies the index and every election file; version alone does not establish identical content. The client requests the index once per page lifetime and caches details by revision and election. It checks revision/version, election, complete unique poll IDs and catalogue counts. Requests time out after 30 seconds; failed requests are evicted for retry, never treated as an empty dataset. Details display loading/error states without previous evidence. Import and build regenerate these assets.

Main-page URLs use /elections/<id> for single elections and /comparison for cross-election comparison. The native History API stores the complete validated view under history.state.pollingHistory = { version: 1, view }; filters, sorting and selection are never encoded into new query strings or hashes. The `showTrajectory` boolean defaults to true when absent or invalid, and is reset to true on election navigation. Reload and popstate restore only supported snapshots matching the current route. Copying a clean URL into a new tab or sharing it opens the page defaults. Discrete changes push history even when the pathname is unchanged; search replaces the current entry. Identical route and snapshot updates do nothing. Initialization and route/state repairs replace history. Other history.state keys are preserved. Dialogs, catalogue pagination, chart viewport and layout preferences are excluded; restoring closes details and never requests table-origin panning. Index, detail and complete-download requests use root-relative /data/ paths so direct nested-route visits work.

Legacy query links remain readable with native URLSearchParams: page=single/comparison, election=<id>, polls=closing/history, ratio=raw/normalized, within30=0/1, q=<search>, sort=brand/date/mae, dir=asc/desc, selected=official or poll:<id>, repeated brands=<brand-id>, and comparison-only min5=0/1. On a valid slash route, the route determines the page and election; legacy query values supply the remaining conditions. Root and /index.html links may also determine the destination through legacy page/election values. After import, replaceState removes the query and hash while retaining the imported view in history. Unknown routes ignore legacy query state and fall back to the home election. Missing or invalid values use the selected page/election defaults. Comparison defaults both within30Days and minimumFiveElections to true; single-election defaults the date filter off and ignores min5. Entering comparison enables both conditions; clicking its active navigation item preserves them. brandIds=null means all, [] means none, and an array of valid IDs selects that subset. Unknown IDs are discarded; an entirely invalid nonempty subset falls back to all. Invalid, cross-election or filtered selections clear permanently.

The root, /index.html, unknown routes and invalid election IDs canonicalize to /elections/2022-taipei with closing polls, normalized percentages, MAE ascending and the 30-day filter disabled. Initial page loading keeps local-election year folders collapsed, including the home redirect. Comparison score navigation and browser history restoration reveal the destination year. Trailing slashes are removed without adding history. If 2022-taipei is absent from a different dataset, the most recent election is the fallback. Navigation into a 2026 pre-result election resets to history, raw percentages, date descending, no 30-day filter, all brand options and no selection. Navigation into completed elections retains the current date filter. Same-tab reload, history restoration and imported legacy links retain their validated settings; comparison score navigation still enters history and selects the exact poll, with the selection stored only in the destination history entry.

Chart colors use the election's official recommending-party classification, not inferred personal membership. The combined independent/not-party-recommended category is neutral gray: 2012 James Soong registered by petition, and 2022 Vivian Huang appears in the same combined category. Historical ternary result stars remain red; pre-result regions have no result meaning. Source references are linked in the application methodology.

The single-election table marks the highest support among the candidates displayed in each poll or official-result row. Comparisons use unrounded values and include all ties. Missing, nonfinite, negative, over-100 or all-zero candidate support prevents marking; invalid original support cannot become a leader through normalization. Leading cells reuse the chart's candidate-region fill at 22% opacity, with dark text, bold values and screen-reader text. Independent and unknown classifications use gray. The official row retains its amber identity and selected rows retain their outline. The same marking applies within the existing two-line layout at a table-container width of 760px or less. The cross-election MAE table has no party-color marking.

The methodology dialog starts with four short explanations for calculation, cross-election interpretation, charts and source limitations. Native collapsed sections retain full calculation and missing-data rules, final-poll/date selection, chart and official-party classification, brand identity evidence and portrait licenses. Single-election detail-drawer background clicks clear selection while keeping the drawer open; text, links, controls and dragging retain selection. The outer backdrop clears selection and closes that single-election drawer.

## Poll-brand filters

機構選項取該場全部紀錄的民調品牌，不受波次、30 天或機構篩選影響。品牌依 src/publisher-brand.mjs 的來源別名與逐筆歸戶裁決判定。選單搜尋比對品牌、品牌別名及獨立 searchTerms 內的原執行者與已確認名稱；執行者搜尋關聯不參與品牌身分歸戶。同一執行者可對應多個品牌，不能因此合併。

初始全選、切場重設全選，null 是全選，[] 是全不選。全選及取消全選作用於全場品牌，搜尋只縮小選項；篩選同步單場圖表與表格，官方結果不受影響。舊 q 搜尋保留原品牌、執行者與原 ID 比對；下拉搜尋只留記憶體。排除已選紀錄永久取消選取，恢復篩選不自動重選，也不開詳情或平移。

單場最新透過 selectSingleElectionPolls 的 institutionKey 按歸戶品牌選取；history 保留全部波次。跨場每品牌每場最多一筆，沿用封關、日期及同優先支持度衝突規則。原 institutionId、poll ID、品牌、支持度、單筆評分、來源與核實資格不改；executor 的未知值不補成品牌。詳情分別顯示歸戶品牌、原收錄品牌、原執行者及其他角色。

history version 1 與 brandIds 欄名保持不變，現在儲存 brand: ID。舊 executor: ID 依當場全部紀錄轉為對應品牌，可一對多；executor:unknown 對應該場未知執行者紀錄所屬品牌。無有效對應的非空篩選回到全選，明確 [] 保持全不選。混合有效品牌與舊執行者 ID 時取有效品牌聯集。原有效品牌、搜尋、排序與符合當前條件的原民調選取仍可還原。
