# Public data contract

Only public and explicitly provenance-labeled evidence belongs in `public/data/verified.json`. The released dataset contains verified primary records AND clearly flagged pending records. Pending records may show provisional arithmetic, but never count as verified analysis. Each record retains public source evidence and the exact verification tier. Test fixtures live under `tests/` and must never be copied to the public bundle.

Additional public collections: `historicalContexts` and `heldRecords` retain original candidate scenarios and source conflicts, each with public `sources`, original support and date observations, reason, pending status and `officialScoreEligible: false`. They never enter `polls` or scoring.

Top-level fields: `version` (string), `updatedAt` (ISO date), `elections` (array), `institutions` (array), `polls` (array), `corrections` (array).

Election: `id`, `label`, `year`, `date` (YYYY-MM-DD), `region`, `office`, `status` (`verified` or `pending`), `candidates` (`id`, `name`, optional `party`, `color`), optional `comparisonCandidateIds` (explicit matched subset, otherwise all candidates), and `result` with `status`, `votes` (candidate-id map of nonnegative numbers or null), `validVotes`, and `source`.

Institution: `id`, `brand`, optional `teamNote`. Each poll separately records `commissioner`, `executor`, `teamId`, `teamNote`; a brand is not assumed to identify a permanent execution team.

The comparison UI groups exact institution brand names and explicitly enumerated aliases without rewriting this dataset. All brands are included automatically. Each displayed brand/election cell selects at most one formally eligible poll using the existing final-poll and conflict rules; the CSV retains that poll's original institution ID. Available per-election scores remain visible even without a universal common set. Cross-brand averages require at least three elections shared by every included brand and never use pending arithmetic.

Poll: `id`, `electionId`, `institutionId`, `fieldworkStart`, `fieldworkEnd`, `publishedAt`, `status` (`verified` or `pending`), `source`, `shares` (candidate-id map, percentage points 0–100 or null), `undecided` (number or null), `other` (number or null), `isExplicitFinal` (boolean), optional `closingEvidence` (public source proving final-poll wording), optional `sampleSize`, `method`, `notes`, `commissioner`, `executor`, `teamId`, `teamNote`.

Source tier: raw source records keep `verification_status='verified_primary'` or `verification_status='indexed_secondary'`, mapped to public `poll.status='verified'` or `poll.status='pending'`. The `provenance` object preserves the original source tier, raw table reference, and audit notes. Indexed rows may have `publishedAt: null` if publication date is not independently verified. Pending may have original full text, news, secondary-table or search-excerpt evidence. The legacy enum `indexed_secondary` means pending verification; it does not assert that every source is secondary. `historical_import.evidence_tier` and each source access method describe the actual evidence. Until six core fields (support, fieldwork end, pre-election publication, executor, sample size and method) have primary evidence and consistency checks pass, **they receive no official MAE, final selection, or cross-election weight**. `sourceCalculation` is retained for provenance; displayed trial arithmetic recomputes the recorded inputs through the shared function. The official `evaluation.mae` remains `null` on pending records. Pending records are visible in the all-records view and may be exported with separate score and review status fields.

Source: `title`, `url` (public https/http URL), optional `publishedAt`, `accessedAt`.

`verificationReview` is independent of `poll.status` and arithmetic: `auditId`, `status` (`primary_verified`, `secondary_cross_checked`, `partial`, `blocked`, `conflict`), `checkedAt`, `reason`, `verifiedFields`, `missingFields`, and `fieldEvidence` keyed by input field. Each field locator has a public `url`, `locator`, `checkedAt`, and optional `note`. It also preserves `originalPublicRecordId`, original observations, per-field corrections, conflicts, recovery attempts, and related record IDs. Primary promotions and conflict adjudications in this round require an approved independent content reread. Two independent complete secondary sources may establish `secondary_cross_checked`; copied wire/press-release text is one source family and does not qualify. This secondary status does not grant an official score.

`calculatePollError(poll, election)` is the shared pure arithmetic function. It normalizes recorded support and official integer-vote distributions separately over the declared comparison set. It returns `calculable`, `mae`, both normalized distributions, `reason`, and `warnings`. It does not require known dates, samples, method, executor, or verification. Incompatible candidate scenarios, missing support, invalid votes, non-voting-intention products, and explicit unresolved support-input conflicts are not calculable. `evaluatePoll` preserves official `eligible`/`mae` semantics and adds `calculation`, `provisionalMae`, `calculationReason`, and `calculationWarnings`. Pending official `mae` remains null. A reported candidate/undecided/other total over 100.5% may retain a provisional MAE with a warning; the official consistency gate remains strict. The original-percentage display never changes either normalized score. The main table defaults to numeric MAE ascending order; unavailable values remain last. Verification details remain available in the selected record's notes and sources.

CSV keeps `mae_percentage_points` for the official score, adding `provisional_mae_percentage_points`, `score_status`, `calculation_reason`, `calculation_warnings` and separate review status/evidence columns. Empty numeric inputs stay empty, never zero. Historical candidate contexts are never scored, even after primary source verification. Unknown fieldwork dates display as unknown and are not assigned to the default 30-day window.

Correction: `date`, `title`, `description`, optional `source`. Do not include private conversation or account information.

MAE is mean absolute difference in percentage points after both distributions are normalized over the exact same declared candidate IDs. Missing candidate support values are ineligible; they are never treated as zero. Raw percentages and excluded candidates remain visible. Election-day and post-election polls are ineligible. The default window includes fieldwork ending 1–30 calendar days before election day; publication must precede election day. Fieldwork start may remain null when not verified; a supplied invalid start is rejected. Explicit final status is accepted only with closingEvidence; otherwise choose the latest verified eligible fieldwork end, then publication date. Conflicting ties require manual review; only exact reprints may use a stable ID to deduplicate. A final poll need not be within seven days.

Cross-election comparison takes one final eligible record per institution/election. All selected institutions use the intersection of eligible elections. An average is shown only for at least three common elections. Per-election values, total coverage and excluded/missing elections are retained.

Calendar interpretation uses Asia/Taipei for timestamps. Date-only values remain calendar dates. Reported non-null candidate values, undecided and other must sum to no more than 100.5%; the 0.5 percentage-point tolerance allows published rounding. Do not invent missing categories to complete 100%.

Historical provenance: `indexDate` and `sourceDateText` are index observations, never substitute fieldwork or publication dates. Unknown executor remains null. `sampleSize` is the per-survey/locality total; `sampleSizeSupportQuestion` is the support question subgroup; `surveyTotalSampleSize` is a separate whole-survey or multi-city total; its precise denominator remains in `supportDenominator` and the field evidence. `otherResponses` preserves named residual categories alongside numeric `other`. `questionCandidateSet` preserves the full question options without inventing zero support for unreported candidates. Candidate spelling aliases preserve the original observations. Catalog accounting uses `auditDisposition` to retain the original audit partition even after adjudication.
